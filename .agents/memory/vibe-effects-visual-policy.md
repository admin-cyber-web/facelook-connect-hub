---
name: Vibe effects visual policy
description: The requested visual direction and timing for Flicks India vibe overlays.
---

Vibe overlays should feel premium, modern, immersive, and realistic rather than like thin borders or simple sparkles. Seasonal effects should match their subject—fireworks, storm rain and lightning, warm sunrise light, and Holi powder—and play vibrantly for the first five seconds.

**Why:** The user explicitly set this direction for the Flicks India Smart Cinematic Vibe engine.

**How to apply:** Keep new or revised vibe effects media-bound, visually prominent, effect-specific, and limited to the initial five-second burst.
